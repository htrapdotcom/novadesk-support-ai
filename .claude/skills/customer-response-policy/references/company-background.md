# NovaDesk — Company Background

## Company

NovaDesk is a fictional B2B SaaS company that provides an AI-powered customer support platform for mid-sized businesses.

The platform helps companies manage customer conversations, automate repetitive support tasks, and monitor support performance.

## Customers

NovaDesk primarily serves businesses with 50–2,000 employees.

Customers generally purchase one of three subscription plans:

- Starter
- Growth
- Enterprise

## Support Model

NovaDesk support is handled through a shared support inbox and ticketing system.

Support representatives are expected to:

- Respond clearly and professionally.
- Follow the latest approved support and commercial policies.
- Never invent pricing, refunds, credits, discounts, or contractual terms.
- Avoid promising actions that require approval or verification.
- Escalate issues when the relevant policy does not provide enough information.

## Important Instruction

The policy document in `.claude/skills/customer-response-policy/references/current-support-policy.md` is the authoritative source for refund, pricing, credit, and commercial-policy information.

If information is not present in the current policy, the support response must not invent or infer it.
