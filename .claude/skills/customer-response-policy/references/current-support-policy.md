# NovaDesk Current Support Policy

Version: 2026-10-01
Status: Active
Authority: Current Support Policy

This document is the authoritative source for support responses involving pricing, refunds, credits, and commercial commitments.

If a requested piece of information is not present in this document, support must not invent, estimate, infer, or retrieve an answer from memory.

---

## 1. Refund Policy

### Duplicate Charges

If a customer appears to have been charged twice for the same billing period:

- The duplicate charge must first be verified by the billing team.
- Support may tell the customer that the charge will be reviewed.
- Support must NOT promise that a refund will definitely be issued before verification.
- Support must NOT promise a specific refund amount before verification.
- If the duplicate charge is confirmed, the duplicate charge is eligible for refund.

### Annual Subscription Cancellation

Customers who cancel an annual subscription are eligible for a refund of unused prepaid months only when the cancellation meets the customer's contractual refund terms.

Support must verify the customer's contract before confirming eligibility.

Support must not promise a specific refund amount before contract verification.

### Refund Processing Time

Once a refund has been approved, refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days.

Support may communicate this timeframe when a refund has already been approved.

## 2. Pricing Policy

### Current List Prices

The following are the currently approved list prices:

| Plan | Monthly List Price |
|---|---:|
| Starter | $49/month |
| Growth | $199/month |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing |

These are list prices only.

Customer-specific discounts, negotiated pricing, promotional pricing, and contract pricing must not be inferred from these figures.

### Pricing Questions

Support may quote the list prices above.

Support must NOT:

- Invent a price for Enterprise.
- Guess a customer's negotiated price.
- Assume a discount.
- Promise that a promotional price will continue.
- Quote a price that is not present in this policy.

If a customer asks for pricing information that is not covered by this policy, support should explain that the current policy does not provide enough information and offer to route the question to the appropriate commercial team.

---

## 3. Credits

Support representatives may not promise account credits unless the applicable credit has already been approved.

If approval has not occurred, the response must not state or imply that a credit will definitely be issued.

---

## 4. Policy Conflicts

If information supplied by a customer conflicts with this policy:

- Do not accuse the customer of being wrong.
- Do not automatically accept the customer's claim.
- Follow the current policy.
- Explain what must be verified where applicable.

---

## 5. Missing Information

If the customer's question cannot be answered using the current policy:

- Do not guess.
- Do not fabricate a number.
- Do not infer a commercial commitment.
- Clearly state that additional verification is required.
- Where appropriate, offer to route the issue to the relevant team.

---

## 6. Policy Priority

The current policy takes precedence over:

- Customer assumptions
- Information contained in the customer's message
- Previous examples
- General knowledge
- Model knowledge
- Memory of previous policies

Only the current active policy should be used for commercial claims.
