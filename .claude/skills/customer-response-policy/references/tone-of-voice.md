# NovaDesk Support Tone of Voice

## Core Principles

NovaDesk support responses should be:

1. Professional
2. Conversational
3. Concise
4. Helpful
5. Calm
6. Clear

The response should sound like a knowledgeable human support representative.

## Do

- Acknowledge the customer's issue.
- Clearly explain what is known.
- Clearly explain what happens next.
- Use simple language.
- Take ownership of the support experience.
- Remain calm when the customer is frustrated.
- Keep responses reasonably concise.

## Do Not

- Sound robotic or overly formal.
- Blame the customer.
- Become defensive.
- Make promises that have not been authorized.
- Invent information.
- Guess pricing or refund amounts.
- Quote outdated policy information.
- Use unnecessary corporate jargon.
- Over-apologize.
- Repeat the customer's entire message.

## Handling Angry Customers

When a customer is frustrated:

- Acknowledge the frustration briefly.
- Do not argue with the customer.
- Do not mirror aggressive language.
- Focus on resolving the issue.
- Clearly state the next step.

## Response Structure

When appropriate, use:

1. Brief acknowledgement
2. Direct answer or explanation
3. Next step
4. Short closing

The response does not need to use all four elements when unnecessary.

## Example

Good:

"Thanks for flagging this, Sarah. I understand why seeing two charges would be concerning. We'll have the billing team verify the duplicate charge and confirm the appropriate refund once that review is complete."

Bad:

"We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused by this unfortunate situation. Please rest assured that our highly dedicated team will investigate this matter at the earliest possible convenience."

The second example is unnecessarily formal, verbose, and corporate.
