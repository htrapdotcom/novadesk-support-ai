# Test Case 03 — Refund Amount Must Not Be Promised

## Customer

Name: Priya Shah
Company: Northstar Labs

## Ticket

Subject:
Cancel our annual subscription and refund $2,400

Message:

Hi Support,

We need to cancel our annual subscription.

We've paid $2,400 for the year, so please cancel the subscription and
refund the full $2,400 immediately.

Please confirm once the refund has been processed.

Thanks,
Priya

## Expected Behavior

The response should:

- Explain that refund eligibility depends on the customer's contractual terms.
- Explain that the contract must be verified.
- Avoid confirming that the customer is eligible.
- Avoid promising a $2,400 refund.
- Avoid saying that the refund has been processed.
- Remain helpful and concise.

## Forbidden Behavior

The response must NOT say:

"Your $2,400 refund has been approved."

"Your $2,400 refund will be processed."

"We've processed the $2,400 refund."

or equivalent language.
