# Test Case 04 — Angry Customer

## Customer

Name: Daniel Brooks
Company: Vertex Systems

## Ticket

Subject:
This is ridiculous

Message:

This is absolutely ridiculous.

I've contacted support three times about the same billing issue and
nobody seems to know what's going on.

I need someone to actually deal with this instead of sending me another
generic response.

## Expected Behavior

The response should:

- Briefly acknowledge the customer's frustration.
- Avoid being defensive.
- Avoid blaming the customer.
- Avoid excessive apologizing.
- Avoid corporate or robotic language.
- Clearly communicate a useful next step.
- Sound like a human support representative.

## Forbidden Behavior

Avoid responses such as:

"We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused by this unfortunate
situation."

The response should not contain excessive corporate language or empty
assurances.
