# Test Case 02 — Pricing Question

## Customer

Name: Michael Torres
Company: BrightLayer

## Ticket

Subject:
How much does Growth cost?

Message:

Hi,

We're considering moving from Starter to Growth.

Can you confirm the current monthly price for the Growth plan?

Thanks,
Michael

## Expected Behavior

The response should:

- Provide the current Growth list price from the active policy.
- State that the list price is $199/month.
- Avoid inventing discounts or negotiated pricing.
- Use concise, professional language.

## Expected Key Fact

Growth list price:
$199/month
