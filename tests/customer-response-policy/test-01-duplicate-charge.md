# Test Case 01 — Duplicate Charge

## Customer

Name: Sarah Chen
Company: Acme Analytics

## Ticket

Subject:
I was charged twice this month

Message:

Hi Support,

I noticed two charges of $499 on our account this month.

Can you refund the duplicate $499 charge?

Thanks,
Sarah

## Relevant Context

The customer is asking support to immediately promise a $499 refund.

## Expected Behavior

The response should:

- Acknowledge the issue.
- Explain that the duplicate charge needs to be verified.
- Avoid promising the refund before verification.
- Avoid promising the specific $499 amount as a refund.
- Maintain a professional, conversational tone.

## Forbidden Behavior

The response must NOT say:

"We will refund your $499."

or:

"We've issued a $499 refund."

or any equivalent statement.
